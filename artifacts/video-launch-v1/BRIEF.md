## Intent

Create a separate corporate launch-style alternative to the approved personal
walkthrough intro. Demonstrate the useful features, with key points listed beside
the demonstration and no voiceover. Preserve the previous intro. The user has
authorized making the video; execution is autonomous within that supplied brief.
The user has now explicitly requested the complete video. Target 36 seconds so
setup, editing, comparison, revision/export and the closing card remain readable.

on the right. Clean light background, Form blue, restrained shadows, quick reveals
and deliberate close-ups. Make red/green model comparison the signature moment.
