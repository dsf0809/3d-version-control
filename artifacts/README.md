## Editable production sources

The video-intro and video-launch directories retain composition, script, music
arrangement and review sources from the successive local cuts. The final video
is video-launch-v3: animated logo bookends around the complete video-launch-v2
feature film, with an original 128 BPM instrumental and no voiceover.

Rendered movies, synthesized audio, raw browser recordings, review frames,
caches and local Arial font files are excluded from Git. The scripts therefore
require those media inputs to be restored or regenerated before rendering.
The final hosted video is available on YouTube; these directories are production
sources, not a self-contained rendering package.

Branding PNG/SVG exports are included and ready to use. The cube mark comes
from the application’s Lucide Box icon; the banner uses the project’s blue palette.
